📝 In-game text
Story, mission, menu & HUD strings baked into the ROM. Font is UPPER-CASE only; edits rewrite the ASM — rebuild (🔧) to apply. In story texts use \n=new line, \f=next page. HUD names have a fixed on-screen width (≈5–8 glyphs) — longer names may overflow the box.

🔒 Codec / progression gates
A radio call (reading a text) sets a story flag; a gated item won't spawn and a gated door won't open until that flag is set. Use the general-pool flags (Flag 0–8) to wire any call to any item/door — free saved flags not tied to the original story. Edits rewrite the ASM tables — rebuild (🔧) to apply. Antenna-gated rooms are edited per-room via the 📡 zone control.
